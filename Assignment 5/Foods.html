Description of Indian Dishes

Rajma Chawal
A popular North Indian dish prepared with kidney beans, tomato, butter and spices.
Biryani
A rice-based dish prepared with aromatic spices and vegetables or meat.
Masala Dosa
A South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter with a potato filling.
