Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a famous historical monument in Agra. It is known for its beautiful architecture and history.

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi and is an important landmark of the city.

Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort in Delhi. It is famous for its red sandstone architecture.

Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is famous for its beautiful backwaters, greenery, beaches and natural landscapes.

Goa Beaches

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches and attracts many tourists.
