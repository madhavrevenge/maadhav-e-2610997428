Delhi

Delhi is the capital of India. It is famous for its historical monuments, markets and modern attractions.

Goa

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, natural scenery and attractive tourist places.

Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station surrounded by mountains. It is a popular destination for tourists.

Jaipur

Jaipur is known as the Pink City. It is famous for forts, palaces and traditional culture.

Kerala

Kerala is famous for its backwaters, greenery, beaches and beautiful natural scenery.
